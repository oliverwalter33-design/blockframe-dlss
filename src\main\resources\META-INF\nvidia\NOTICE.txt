NVIDIA DLSS / DLAA for Minecraft 26.2

This mod bundles unmodified redistributable binaries from NVIDIA Streamline SDK
2.12.0, NVIDIA DLSS 310.7.0 and the Vulkan low-latency runtime required by the
official Streamline distribution. Those NVIDIA components remain subject to
NVIDIA's licenses included next to this notice and in the native resource
directory. They are not licensed under the MIT license for the mod code and may
not be extracted or redistributed as stand-alone products.

The mod's Java/JNI integration code is independent experimental software and is
not endorsed by NVIDIA or Mojang.
